Route One is a one-person project, so support runs through email — but that also means a real reply, not a ticket queue.

Common questions

How does scoring work?

Each route starts at full points. Every hint you reveal — nationality, position, a club name — costs points. Guess correctly before you run out of hints for the highest score on that player.

Why don't I recognise a club crest?

Crests in Route One are procedurally generated rather than official club badges, so the shapes and colours are inspired by the club's identity but won't match the real thing exactly.

Where does the career data come from?

Every route is sourced from English Wikipedia. If you spot an error in a player's career — a missing club, wrong dates, anything — flag it and it'll get corrected.

Does Route One need an internet connection?

No. The whole app, including the map and all player data, is bundled on your device. It works fully offline.

Is there a way to sync progress across devices?

Not currently — progress is stored locally on each device. It's something being considered for a future update.
